# Your EFDS committee account

**For EFDS committee members and account administrators · September 2026**

Use your own account. Everyone starts as a **member**, with full event access and public resources. An EFDS administrator can verify society membership, making you an **EFDS member** with access to member resources when they are published. Committee access and your officer roster identity are two further, separate steps.

## For committee members

1. **Create your account.** Visit [imperial-efds.com/signup](https://www.imperial-efds.com/signup). Use your own `@imperial.ac.uk` or `@ic.ac.uk` address. If you serve on the committee with a different address, ask an EFDS administrator to approve that address first. Select **Email me an account link**, open the newest email, press **Continue**, and set a password. Email ownership confirmation gives you basic member dashboard access immediately; society verification is a later, separate review. You can also use an email sign-in link, or Google sign-in if its button is available. Microsoft sign-in is not offered.
2. **Finish from your inbox.** Open the newest EFDS email, then press **Continue** on the EFDS confirmation page. Set and confirm a password if prompted. Opening the link alone does not sign you in; the extra step protects against automatic email-security scans. If the link has expired, request a new one. Do not share it.
3. **Request society verification.** Open [Your profile](https://www.imperial-efds.com/dashboard/profile). Under **EFDS membership**, describe how you are connected to the society and select **Send for review**. Your university email confirms affiliation with Imperial, but does not by itself prove society membership. Your member account still has full event access while the review is pending.
4. **Tell the administrator your committee identity.** In your usual committee channel, share your full name, the exact account email, your official committee title and academic year. Never share a password, email sign-in link or reset code.
5. **Check your access.** After an administrator confirms verification, committee access and your officer link, refresh [Your profile](https://www.imperial-efds.com/dashboard/profile). It should show **Committee** and the correct name, title and academic year. Open [Tickets](https://www.imperial-efds.com/dashboard/tickets) to see committee work. Ask the administrator to correct any mismatch; do not create a second account.

**Keep one account.** Signing in with a different email can create a separate identity. The account and officer roster entry are what connect you to ticket assignments.

## For EFDS account administrators

Use the [Accounts review panel](https://www.imperial-efds.com/admin/accounts) after the person signs in once. Check the current society or committee record through a trusted source. A name, student email or self-description alone is not sufficient evidence. The panel records the acting admin, decision and date. It prevents direct role changes, stale edits and two active accounts linking to the same officer entry.

1. **Verify EFDS membership.** In **Needs review**, open the person's exact account. Compare their claim with an independent EFDS record. Select **Verify EFDS membership** and save. If the claim cannot be confirmed, select **Decline verification** and ask the person for corrected details. A declined account remains visible for follow-up.
2. **Grant committee access.** After verification, switch to **All active accounts**, find that account, select **Grant committee access** and save. Verification and promotion are deliberately separate decisions.
3. **Link the correct officer entry.** On the same account, select **Link committee identity** and choose the active officer roster entry with the correct full name, title and academic year. Check [Admin → Committee](https://www.imperial-efds.com/admin/committee) if a roster entry is missing or already linked. The database refuses a duplicate active link.
4. **Check the outcome.** Ask the member to refresh their profile and ticket dashboard. Use the account panel's recent access decisions to confirm what changed and when. Admin promotion cannot be applied to an unverified account. An admin cannot change their own role or remove the last active admin.

The old `grant_access.py` database command remains a bootstrap or recovery tool for an authorised operator; routine account reviews now belong in the audited dashboard. Do not put database credentials in a command, shared document or chat.

## If something does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No email arrives | Check junk mail, confirm the address, wait a few minutes, then request a new email. Ask an admin to inspect delivery health if it still fails. |
| The link opens the homepage or says expired | Request a fresh link and open the newest one. Send an admin the error text, never the link or its code. |
| You can sign in, but only see member access | Complete the EFDS membership request on your profile; ask an admin to review the exact account email. |
| EFDS member access is active, but Tickets is missing | Ask an admin to grant committee access and link your roster identity. |
| Committee access appears, but your title is wrong | Ask an admin to correct the officer link. Do not make another account. |
| Password sign-in fails | Use **Forgot password?** or an email sign-in link. |
| Google sign-in is unavailable | Use email sign-in. Google requires separate provider configuration. |

EFDS is a student society. Its account access is managed by the society and is separate from Imperial College London's account permissions.
